**How the agent works:**
- Code builds every debt reading as a fixed Turkish sentence:
  - amounts in words ("beþ bin lira on üç kuruþ")

  - full currency names
  - the reading order the bank set
- Dates are decided from `now`.
- The model handles card choice, the offer, sending the statement by e-mail and the statement-week change.
- Where the bank's documents disagreed, the newest one won. Each conflict and its winner is listed in `out/final.rules.md`, and `out/final.audit.md` quotes the line that enforces each rule.
